### 8.2 Typy položek

Secret položka používá typ:

- `PASSWORD`
- `API_KEY`
- `BEARER_TOKEN`
- `OAUTH_CLIENT`
- `OAUTH_TOKEN_SET`
- `TOTP_SEED`
- `CERTIFICATE`
- `PRIVATE_KEY`
- `WEBHOOK_SECRET`
- `DATABASE_CREDENTIAL`
- `SESSION_STATE`
- `COOKIE_JAR`
- `SSH_CREDENTIAL`
- `GENERIC_TEXT`
- `GENERIC_BINARY`
